Why geography matters

Program impact has a location. Now your data does too.

Most MEL tools track numbers. DELTA also shows you where those numbers come from — mapping indicator performance, field survey locations, and project sites onto interactive maps that update as your program progresses.

⌖
Indicators mapped by geography
See indicator performance distributed across regions, districts, and localities — with color-coded overlays that reveal where results are strong and where attention is needed.
⊙
Field data GPS visualization
GPS coordinates collected in field forms appear automatically on the map — beneficiary locations, site visits, and infrastructure points plotted without manual re-entry.
⊞
External GIS layers
Overlay data from external geographic systems to enrich your program maps with contextual information — administrative boundaries, infrastructure, or environmental data.
⟁
Connected to your MEL data
Maps in DELTA update as indicators are tracked and field data is validated — giving managers a geographic view of program progress without any separate GIS workflow.
Core capabilities

Geographic intelligence, built into MEL.

⌖
Indicator performance maps
Visualize any indicator's performance as a geographic distribution — with color intervals that show achievement relative to targets at any administrative level your program uses.
  • Drill down from country to village level
  • Color-coded by performance range
  • Disaggregated data visible on the map
⊙
Field data on the map
GPS data collected in field forms — point coordinates, field trajectories, and geographic zones — appears on the map automatically as submissions are validated.
  • GPS coordinates mapped per submission
  • Field trajectories and area polygons supported
  • Popup details per location point
⊞
Layered geographic context
Combine your program data with external geographic layers — administrative boundaries, infrastructure networks, environmental data — for richer spatial analysis and reporting.
  • External GIS system integration supported
  • Grayscale, street map, and satellite basemaps
  • Layers toggled on and off per indicator
⌁
Portfolio geographic view
See all projects in your portfolio plotted on a single map — color-coded by sector, status, or investment level — giving headquarters and donors an instant geographic overview of where programs are active.
  • All projects mapped on the portfolio home
  • Color-coded by sector or performance
  • Hover for project summary at a glance

Why it matters

GIS built into MEL,
not bolted on.

Most organizations use GIS as a separate tool, disconnected from their MEL data. In DELTA, geographic visualization is part of the same platform — so maps update as field teams submit data and indicators are tracked, with no export-import workflow required.

⌖
No separate GIS workflow
Maps update automatically as program data changes — no export to a separate mapping tool, no manual geo-referencing, no version management.
⊙
Field GPS to map in one step
GPS data collected in field forms appears on the map as submissions are validated — giving managers a geographic view of field activity without any additional steps.
⊞
Extend with external data
Bring in geographic layers from external systems to add context — infrastructure, administrative boundaries, environmental conditions — overlaid on your program's own data.
