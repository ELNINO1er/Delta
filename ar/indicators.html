Why it's different

Indicators connected to program reality.

DELTA is not a simple indicator register. It helps manage indicator logic within a broader program environment that includes planning, data collection, dashboards, and reporting.

Built around the logframe — the standard used by USAID, World Bank, EU, and AfDB. Indicators live exactly where auditors will look.
