The challenge

Implementation oversight breaks down without structure.

When activities aren't connected to the results chain, financial data sits in separate spreadsheets, and reporting relies on manual compilation — implementation visibility suffers. DELTA brings planning, tracking, and reporting into one structured environment.

⊟
Structured activity planning
Plan activities at the level that fits your program cycle — global, annual, or period — with budget and targets integrated from the start.
∑
Financial visibility built in
Track budget vs actuals, financing sources, and disbursements alongside physical progress — no separate financial tracking required.
⟁
Connected to your results chain
Activities are linked to logframe elements — so implementation progress always connects back to the indicators and outcomes it supports.
⊞
Reporting without rebuilding
Built-in dashboards and reports give managers and donors current implementation status — without exporting to Excel and starting over.
Core capabilities

Everything implementation oversight requires.

⊟
Multi-level activity planning
Structure your activity plan across the planning horizons your program uses — from multi-year frameworks down to quarterly or monthly schedules. Revised planning supported throughout the program cycle.
  • Global, annual, and period planning levels
  • Budget per financing source, by location
  • Revised planning with full audit trail
∑
Financial tracking & disbursements
Monitor commitments, disbursements, and spending by financing source and agreement — with multi-currency support and budget performance graphs built into every activity.
  • Budget vs actuals, by location and period
  • Multi-currency with exchange rate management
  • Disbursement tracking by financing source
⌁
Timeline & milestones
Visualize the full project timeline with milestones — with at-a-glance status on what's on schedule and what needs attention, across the whole program.
  • Project timeline with milestone tracking
  • Progress visualization across all activities
  • Portfolio-level cross-cutting activities
⊞
Built-in reporting
Activity dashboards, progress reports, financial tables, and staff reports — all available directly from the activity module. Export to Word, Excel, or PDF for donor submission.
  • Activity progress & financial dashboards
  • Custom reports per activity or program-wide
  • Export Word · Excel · PDF
