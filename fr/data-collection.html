Core capabilities

Structured by design.
Flexible by nature.

12×
12 field types, no code required
Build forms that match the complexity of your program — from simple text and numbers to GPS coordinates, photo capture, Likert score fields, calculated formulas, and geospatial paths. Drag, drop, configure.
  • Text, Number, Date, List, Choice, Score fields
  • GPS coordinates, Path, Polygon for geospatial data
  • Picture and Audio attachment fields
  • Calculation field — automatic formulas between fields
⇄
Conditional logic & skip patterns
Show or hide fields based on what respondents select. Ask the right questions to the right people — without creating separate forms for every scenario. Templates are reusable across projects.
  • Skip logic based on field values
  • Required and conditional field configuration
  • Multi-section forms with named tabs
  • Save forms as templates — reuse across the portfolio
◎
Review, validate & approve
Don't let bad data reach your indicators. Assign validation workflows with multiple steps — collection, verification, approval — and assign each step to a specific team member with notifications.
  • Validation statuses: Valid, In process, Invalid, To delete
  • Multi-step workflow: collect → verify → approve
  • Assignable steps with notification badges
  • Filter, export, and audit trail per record
⟁
Connected to your logframe
Form fields can be linked to logframe elements, disaggregation categories, stakeholders, and locations — so every approved record contributes directly to indicator progress and geospatial visualization.
  • Link forms directly to logframe indicators
  • KPI disaggregation fields (gender, location, age…)
  • Location fields feed GIS visualization directly
  • Analyze & Report built into the module
How it works

Four steps. One continuous flow.

1
Form design
Build the form your program needs.
Use DELTA's drag-and-drop builder to design forms with 12 field types, conditional logic, and validation rules. Organize fields into sections, set required fields, and save as a template for reuse across your portfolio.
2
Field collection
Online, offline, wherever your teams are.
Choose the mode that fits each situation — on-screen entry for supervisors, the mobile app for field agents with or without connectivity, a shareable link for external respondents, or bulk import from Excel or KoBo.
3
Validation
Catch errors before they reach reporting.
Supervisors review incoming submissions, assign validation statuses, and approve records. Multi-step workflows route records through collection, verification, and approval — with notifications at each step.
4
Results linkage
Approved records become indicator progress.
Validated records flow into your logframe — updating indicator actuals, refreshing dashboards, and feeding donor reports. Analytics are built directly into the module, no separate tool required.

Why it matters

More than a
standalone form tool.

DELTA's data collection module is not a survey app. It is a structured collection environment that sits inside a full MEL platform — where every record captured contributes to results tracking, geographic visualization, and institutional reporting.

01
No re-entry, no spreadsheets
Data collected in the field flows automatically into your indicators and dashboards. What field teams capture in the morning is visible to headquarters by afternoon — structured, validated, and mapped to your logframe.
02
Reliability you can report on
A validation workflow with configurable steps, assignable roles, and status tracking means the data reaching your donors has been reviewed and approved — not just collected and assumed to be correct.
03
Built for institutional accountability
Every submission has an audit trail. Every validation status is recorded. Every record links to a form, a project, a logframe element. The structure that institutional donors and governments expect is built in by design.
